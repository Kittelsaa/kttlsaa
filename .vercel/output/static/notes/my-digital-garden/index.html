Welcome to my Digital Garden, I have gotten used to the term ‘digital gardening’ to describe my notes taking process. A “digital garden” is an analogy/framework used to take more intentional notes. Digital gardening is not just writing notes, but actively finding links between different pieces of knowledge.

I have really enjoyed using this system to build my own personal private digital garden using obsidian, but I’m taking a step further by gardening in public, sharing some of my notes & ideas in public, which is a core principles of digital gardening.

I’m exploring two core domains in this digital garden. First: Tech, I’m a software engineer tracking the industry’s evolution & critical turning points is really important to me.How digital technologies shape human cultures, behaviors, and how we shape them in return. Second: Philosophy; the study of fundamental questions concerning topics like knowledge, mind, reason — anyone who knows me understands this is where my deepest curiosity lies. It is an essential tool for social and practical problem-solving.

I will be documenting my findings over here & you can always access or use the repository of this project here to build your own digital garden.
